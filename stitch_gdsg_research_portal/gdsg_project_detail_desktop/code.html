Active Research Project

Urban Heat Island Mitigation Modeling

Deploying high-resolution sensor networks and predictive AI to map thermal disparities and optimize localized cooling interventions in high-density metropolitan areas.

insights Project Overview

The Urban Heat Island (UHI) effect presents one of the most critical challenges to public health and urban sustainability in the 21st century. This project seeks to move beyond macro-level satellite thermal imaging by establishing a hyper-local, multi-modal data infrastructure capable of modeling microclimate dynamics at the street level.

Our primary objective is to develop a predictive framework that not only identifies high-risk thermal zones but also simulates the impact of specific mitigation strategies—such as increased canopy cover, high-albedo surfaces, and optimized wind corridors. By integrating real-time sensor telemetry with historical meteorological datasets, we aim to provide urban planners with actionable, granular insights.

Sensor Deployment

Deployment of 500+ low-cost IoT environmental nodes across stratified urban typologies. Nodes capture temperature, humidity, and particulate matter at 5-minute intervals.

Hardware: LoRaWAN, DHT22
memory

Predictive Modeling

Implementation of Spatiotemporal Graph Convolutional Networks (STGCN) to forecast thermal anomalies up to 72 hours in advance, incorporating structural urban morphology data.

Framework: PyTorch Geo
