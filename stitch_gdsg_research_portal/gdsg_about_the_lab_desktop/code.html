About Our Lab

Advancing geospatial science through rigorous inquiry.

The Geospatial Data Science Group (GDSG) is dedicated to unraveling complex spatial phenomena through advanced computational methods and interdisciplinary collaboration.

explore

Our Mission

To develop robust, scalable, and equitable geospatial analytical frameworks that address critical global challenges. We strive to push the boundaries of spatial data science by integrating machine learning, high-performance computing, and fundamental geographic principles.

visibility

Our Vision

A future where spatial intelligence seamlessly integrates into decision-making processes, fostering sustainable environments and resilient communities worldwide.

Research Philosophy

science

Scientific Excellence

We prioritize methodological rigor and reproducible research. Our computational pipelines are designed for transparency and peer validation.

hub

Interdisciplinary Collaboration

Complex spatial problems require diverse perspectives. We actively partner with domain experts in ecology, urban planning, and public health.

code

Open Science

We are committed to the open source community. Our datasets, models, and analytical tools are publicly accessible to foster global innovation.
