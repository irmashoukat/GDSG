Organization Overview
Geospatial Data Science Group (GDSG) is a research-focused organization working in the fields of Geographic Information Systems (GIS), Geospatial Data Science, Spatial Analytics, Remote Sensing, GeoAI, Machine Learning, Earth Observation, and Data Visualization.
The organization promotes interdisciplinary research, develops innovative geospatial technologies, collaborates with universities and industry, publishes scientific research, and provides education and training in geospatial sciences.
The website should present GDSG as a modern research institute rather than a commercial software company.

To advance geospatial science through innovative research, artificial intelligence, spatial analytics, and interdisciplinary collaboration.
